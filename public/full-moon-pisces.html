Full Sturgeon Moon
Pisces Eclipse Ceremony

A moment to release, surrender, and let the eclipse illuminate what is ready to go.

Opening

Tonight the sky is doing something rare.

A full Sturgeon Moon in Pisces. A partial lunar eclipse so deep, up to 96% of the Moon will pass through Earth's shadow — creating an almost blood moon visible across the world.

Even here in Australia, where we cannot see the eclipse shadow, we feel it. The Moon still rises. The energy still moves through us.

Pisces is the final sign. The dreamer. The healer. The one who knows that some things cannot be fixed — only released.

This is a ceremony for endings. For what you are finally ready to let go of.

"You cannot begin what is next until you have truly released what is done."

Step 1 of 7

Create your space.

This does not need to be elaborate. It just needs to feel intentional.

If you can, light a candle. Place your feet on the ground. Let your shoulders drop. Close the apps. You are here now.

Take one slow breath in.

Step 2 of 7

What are you ready to release?

Full moon eclipses are portals for endings. They accelerate what is already moving. They illuminate what you have been avoiding looking at directly.

Sit with these questions. You do not need to answer all of them. Let your body tell you which one pulls.

What have I been carrying that was never mine to carry?
What story about myself am I finally tired of believing?
What relationship, habit, or pattern has run its course?
Write what is ready to go
Step 3 of 7

The water ritual.

Pisces is a water sign. Water holds memory. Water also cleanses.

If you have a glass of water nearby, hold it in both hands. If not, simply place both hands on your heart.

Breathe into your hands.

Speak aloud, or whisper, or simply think:

"I release what no longer belongs to me. I return it to the water. I return it to the earth. I am cleansed. I am clear. I am free."

If you have water, take a slow sip when you are ready. Feel it move through you, carrying the old energy with it.

What is the eclipse revealing to you?

Eclipses bring things into the light that have been hidden. Not to hurt us — to free us. The shadow passing across the Moon is a reminder that some truths only become visible in the dark.

Something has shifted in you tonight, even subtly. Something you now see that you could not see before.
