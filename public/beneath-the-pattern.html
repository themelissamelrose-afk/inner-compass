Beneath the Pattern

A companion to this week's Mel's Monday Musings. Your writing saves on this device as you go.

Before You Begin
Come home to the body first.
Put both feet on the floor. One hand on your heart. Take three slow breaths. Each time you exhale, let it out with a sigh, and let the body soften.

What have I been trying to solve by doing more? The routines, the plans, the strategies, the next thing I was sure would fix it.

Your Patterns Are Behaviours
You cannot always see a belief. You can see a behaviour, because it repeats.

Tap the ones that feel true for you right now.

overgiving people pleasing overfunctioning overexplaining going quiet achieving and never landing staying busy controlling the outcome rescuing everyone saying yes when I mean no

The one that is loudest this week, and anything else I notice in myself.

The last time I caught myself doing it, what was happening around me, and what was I feeling?

Where does it sit in my body? Describe the sensation. How old does it feel?

Underneath the Behaviour Is a Belief
Formed young, before you had words for it.

If I stopped doing this thing, what am I afraid would happen?

Whose voice, or whose life, does that fear echo? Where might I have first learned it?

Safety Is Familiar, Not Comfortable
The body chooses what it knows, even when the known hurts.

What is the payoff of staying exactly as I am? What do I not have to feel, risk, or receive?

What do I actually desire, that my body has never yet experienced? Name it plainly.

Receiving Is a Worthiness Thing
Most of us are not blocked from receiving. We just do not yet believe we are allowed.

Finish this a few times, honestly. I would let myself receive this if...

If I already were worthy of it, with nothing left to prove, what would change today?

The Loving Return
Notice the behaviour. Feel it in the body. Hear the story. Then come home, gently.
It is okay. I know this does not feel safe. You can create this time. We are safe.

Write the words I most need to hear from myself when the old pattern rises.

Your Two Windows
The minutes before you open your eyes, and the minutes before you sleep. Not thinking. Feeling.

In the morning, before you move. What do I want to create today, and what does it feel like in my body?

In the evening, before you sleep. Where did I witness myself today with curiosity instead of criticism?

You showed up for yourself today. That is not small.

You do not need to become someone new. You need to meet the person you already are with a little more love than yesterday. That is the whole practice. Presence over perfection. Big love Melissa x
