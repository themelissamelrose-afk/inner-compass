The Portal Ritual

Tonight the veil is thin, the numbers align, and the door is open. This ritual is an act of power. You are not wishing. You are claiming.

Step 1 of 8 · Cast Your Circle
Begin by creating sacred space.
Before anything else, you need to draw a boundary between the ordinary world and the sacred one. This is what it means to cast a circle, you are declaring that this space, this moment, is set apart. What happens inside it is intentional.

Find a candle if you have one. Light it. If you do not have one, place your hand on your heart and feel its warmth. That flame is always inside you.
When you light the candle, say aloud or in your heart: "I cast this circle in love and in power. Only what serves my highest good may enter here."
Step 2 of 8 · Call In the Elements
Invite the four directions to witness.
Witchcraft works with the intelligence of the natural world. The four elements hold ancient wisdom and they respond when called with intention. Tap each element to call it in. Speak the words aloud as you do.
🜃
Earth · North
"I call in the North, the element of Earth. Ground me in my truth. Anchor what I am calling in."
🜁
Air · East
"I call in the East, the element of Air. Clear my mind. Carry my intentions to the universe."
🜂
Fire · South
"I call in the South, the element of Fire. Activate my power. Burn away what no longer belongs."
🜄
Water · West
"I call in the West, the element of Water. Open my heart. Flow me toward what is mine."
Step 3 of 8 · The Invocation
Speak the portal open.
Words spoken aloud carry a different frequency to words thought in the mind. Tonight you are not wishing. You are declaring. Read the invocation below aloud, slowly, feeling each line in your body.
Read this aloud
I stand at the 11:11 portal, fully present and fully awake.

I call in my highest timeline. I call in the version of my life that is already waiting for me to claim it.

I am not asking for permission. I am not waiting to be chosen. I am stepping forward as the woman who already knows.

The portal is open. I walk through with my whole self. Nothing held back.

So it is.
Step 4 of 8 · The Lineage
Call in the women who healed before you.
The veil between worlds is still thin in Scorpio season. November holds the energy of ancestral connection, of those who came before, of the lineage that runs in your blood.

You are not doing this healing alone. There are women in your lineage who wanted this freedom and could not reach it. Women who carried what you carry, who passed it forward unconsciously, hoping one day someone in the line would stop and choose differently.

You are that woman. What you heal in yourself, you heal in them too.
Speak this aloud or feel it in your heart
I call in the women of my lineage who healed and those who could not.

I honour the strength it took to survive. I honour the love they carried even inside their pain.

I am here now. I am choosing differently. What was passed to me in the dark, I am choosing to meet in the light.

I heal for myself and I heal for them. The line changes here.
Take a moment in silence. If you feel something rise in your body, let it be there. This is the lineage responding.
Step 5 of 8 · Activate the Portal
11 rounds to open the channel.
This is an activation breath. Two short inhales through the nose to draw in power, then one long exhale through the mouth to release and expand. Eleven rounds for 11:11. This breath opens your nervous system and your energy field to receive what you are calling in.
Double inhale through the nose, two short sniffs. Then one long slow exhale through the mouth. Feel the energy building with each round.

Step 6 of 8 · The Bay Leaf Ritual
Write your desire and release it to the universe.
The bay leaf ritual is one of the oldest forms of wish magic. You write your desire, then burn it, sending the energy and intention into the universe. The burning is not destruction, it is transformation. It is saying: I am not holding onto this with my grip. I am trusting the universe to receive it and return it.

Write your deepest desire for this new chapter. One thing, stated simply and clearly, written as if it is already true.
Write your desire as if it is already real
Your desire has been released to the universe. It is received. Trust the unfolding.
Step 7 of 8 · Mirror Work
One truth, spoken to yourself.
Mirror work is one of the most powerful and most confronting practices in witchcraft and in healing. To look into your own eyes and speak truth directly to yourself, without deflecting, without minimising, is an act of radical self-recognition.

If you have a mirror near you, look into your own eyes as you write this. If not, close your eyes and see yourself.

What is the one truth you have been avoiding saying to yourself? Not about what you have done wrong, but about who you actually are. What does the woman on the other side of this portal know about herself that the old version was afraid to claim?

Step 8 of 8 · Seal the Portal
Claim it. Close the circle.
Every circle that is opened must be closed. Every ritual that begins must be sealed. This is not just ceremony, it is energetic completion. You have done the work. Now you seal it in with gratitude and with power.

Thank the elements for their witness. Thank your ancestors for their love. Thank the portal for opening. And step back into your ordinary life carrying everything you have claimed here.
Speak this as your closing
I thank the Earth, the Air, the Fire and the Water for holding this space.

I thank the women of my lineage for their witness and their love.

I seal this intention in the highest good of all.

This circle is open but never broken. The magic continues.

So it is.
The portal has received me. I am already on the other side.
I am not waiting to become her. I am her, choosing to remember.
What I claimed tonight, the universe already knows.
