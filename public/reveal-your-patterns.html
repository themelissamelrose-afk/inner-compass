Reveal Your Patterns
Meditation

Breathwork, nervous system settling, and a gentle return to yourself.

Before anything can shift, you have to see it. Not to judge it. Not to fix it. Just to see it, clearly, with curiosity and without shame.

This is where the journey begins. Module One is not about changing anything yet. It is about becoming the witness. Noticing what has been running quietly in the background of your life, the patterns, the behaviours, the beliefs that were formed long before you had words for them.

The moment you can observe a pattern, you are no longer inside it. That space is where everything becomes possible.

This meditation will guide you back into your body, settle your nervous system, and begin to open the door to what is underneath. Come with curiosity. Come without expectation. Come exactly as you are.

Recommended: at the start of each module, or any time you need to ground before a session.
