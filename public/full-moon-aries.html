Full Harvest Moon
Aries Ceremony

I don't abandon myself to create the WE. A ceremony for claiming what is mine.

Opening

The Harvest Moon is asking a different question this time.

Not "what else do I need to make happen?" But something quieter and more honest.

What has already grown? What is ready to be received? What am I finally willing to stop carrying forward?

This is a Full Moon in Aries. Aries is the sign of the self. Of desire. Of what is mine to initiate. And with the Libra Sun opposite, the axis that holds this ceremony is one of the most personal questions we can ask: have I been abandoning myself to create the WE?

This is not a ceremony for releasing other people. It is a ceremony for coming back to yourself.

"I don't create to be chosen. I create because it is true for me. And I allow what can meet me, to meet me."
Step 1 of 8

Create your space.

This does not need to be elaborate. It just needs to feel intentional.

If you can, light a candle. Aries is a fire sign. The flame is your witness tonight.

Step 2 of 8

The harvest. What has already grown?

Harvest Moon energy is not about striving. It is about receiving what has already come.

Before you look at what needs to change, first look at what has. The growth you have done, even quietly. Even in ways you have not named out loud.

What did I do this year that I didn't think I could?
What shifted inside me, even if nothing outside changed?
What am I no longer doing that used to exhaust me?
Write what has grown in you
Step 3 of 8

What am I no longer willing to carry forward?

The Aries and Libra axis asks us to look honestly at the places we have been doing the work of two. Creating connection where it wasn't being created back. Pursuing what wasn't pursuing us. Initiating where there was no reciprocation.

This is not about blame. It is about honest witnessing.

"What am I ready to stop creating, chasing or carrying because it can only become real when two people participate?"
Write what you are ready to put down
Step 4 of 8

What do I want to create simply because it is mine?

This is the Aries question. Not "what will they want?" Not "what will work?" Not "what will make them stay?"

What do you actually want? What is yours to initiate? Where have you been holding back your desire, your creativity, your sensuality, your voice, because you were waiting for permission, or waiting to feel chosen first?

Aries doesn't wait. Aries creates.

I open the door. I take aligned action. Then I stop pushing. I watch what comes toward me.
What is mine to create, desire, or initiate?
Where am I ready to act from desire rather than from trying to secure a response?
Step 5 of 8

Fire breath.

Aries is ruled by Mars. This breath activates the fire in your body and moves what has been sitting heavy in your chest.

How it works

Two sharp inhales through the nose, one on top of the other, filling the lungs completely.

Then one long, forceful exhale through the mouth. Let it be loud. Let it be a release.

Three rounds. Each one builds the fire. Each exhale is a letting go.

Sit up tall. Relax your jaw. Tap the circle when you are ready.
