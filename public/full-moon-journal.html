Full Moon Journal

No right answers. No performance. Just you, the page, and what is honestly moving through you tonight.

🌊
Arriving
Before we go anywhere, we come home first.
Place one hand on your heart. Take three slow breaths. Notice what is here, without trying to change it.

What is your body holding right now? Not the story around it, just what do you feel, physically, when you check in?

What has your nervous system been bracing for lately? What are you waiting for to be over?

🌕
What Is Ready to Release
Pisces asks us to surrender what we cannot fix, only release.
Eclipses illuminate what the ordinary dark hides. What has been underneath, waiting to be named?

What are you finally ready to put down? Not what you think you should release. What your body is actually tired of carrying?

What story about yourself has run its full course? The one that kept you small, kept you safe, kept you from something?

What have you been carrying that was never actually yours? Whose fear, whose wound, whose belief are you still holding?

✨
What Is Opening
Every ending is the beginning of something truer.

Who are you becoming as this cycle completes? Not who you are supposed to be. Who you actually feel yourself moving toward?

What does your life feel like in your body when you imagine yourself free of what you are releasing?

What is one honest step toward that version of yourself? Not a plan. One step, from the body, that feels true.

You showed up for yourself today.
That is not small.

Bring what came up here into our next session.
Especially the thing you wrote and then almost deleted.
That one is usually the most important.
