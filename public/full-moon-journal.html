For Melissa's 1:1 Clients · Full Moon · Pisces · August 2026

Full Moon Journal

This is your space. No right answers. No performance. Just you, the page, and the honest truth of what is moving through you right now.

Take your time with each prompt. Let the body lead, not the mind.

1
Arriving in Your Body
Before we go anywhere, we come home first.
Place one hand on your heart. Take three slow breaths. Notice what is here, without trying to change it.

What is your body holding right now? Not the story around it, not the explanation — just what do you feel, physically, when you check in?

What has your nervous system been bracing for lately? What are you waiting for to be over?

If your body could send you one message right now, what would it say?

2
The Pattern That Is Completing
Eclipses accelerate what is already moving. Something is ready to end.
You are not broken. You are patterned. And this moon is asking which pattern has finally run its full course.

What pattern do you keep finding yourself in, even though part of you knows better? The relationship dynamic, the way you give until you have nothing left, the voice in your head that says you are too much or not enough.

Where did this pattern begin? Not in this chapter — but in the one before it. What was happening in your life when you first learned this was how things work?

What has this pattern been protecting you from? Underneath the behaviour, what is the fear that was being kept safe?

What would you lose if this pattern ended? Be honest. Sometimes we hold onto what hurts us because the familiar feels safer than the unknown.

3
Your Inner Teen
She built the walls. She holds the door. She needs to be met first.
Your inner teen was not difficult. She was protecting something that needed to be protected. She built her walls because the world asked her to be something she was not, and she did the best she could with what she had.

Cast your mind back to your teenage years. What age comes up first — not the one you choose, the one that appears? What was life like for her? What was happening on the outside, and what was she feeling on the inside that no one knew about?

What did she need that she did not receive? Not just practically — emotionally. What was she hungry for that was never offered?

What mask did she build to survive? The one she wore at school, at home, in friendships — the version of herself she learned to perform so she would be accepted?

As your adult self, what do you want to say to her right now? Not to fix her or explain anything. Just to let her know she has been seen.

4
Your Inner Child
Beneath the teen, there is a younger one waiting to be found.
Your body already knows what your inner child needed. It has been carrying the memory. This is not about going back — it is about finally giving her what she always deserved.

When you picture your younger self — before the years of adapting, before the masks — what do you see? What is she doing, what does she love, what lights her up?

What did she believe about herself that was never true — a belief that was handed to her by someone who did not know how to love her well?

What can you give her right now that she has been waiting for? Not one day, not when things are better. Right now, today, what is the small act of reparenting you can offer her?

5
What This Moon Is Illuminating
Eclipse light shows what you have been unable to see in the ordinary dark.
Pisces is the final sign. It asks us to trust the release. Not to fix, not to understand — to surrender what is no longer alive in us, even when we cannot yet see what comes next.

What is this eclipse bringing into the light for you? Something you have been circling, sensing, but not quite letting yourself name directly.

What are you finally ready to release? Not what you think you should release — what your body and your soul are actually ready to put down?

What have you been carrying that was never actually yours? Whose fear, whose wound, whose belief are you still holding?

6
What Is Opening
Every ending is the beginning of something truer.

Who are you becoming as this cycle completes? Not who you are supposed to be — who you actually feel yourself moving toward?

What does life feel like in your body when you imagine yourself fully free of the pattern that is completing?

What is one honest step you can take this week toward that version of yourself? Not a plan. Not a goal. One step, from the body, that feels true.

✦ You showed up for yourself today. ✦

That is not small.

The pages you have filled are not just words.
They are the parts of you that finally had room to speak.

Take what came up today into our next session.
Bring the questions that are still sitting with you.
Bring the thing you wrote and then almost deleted.

That one is usually the most important.
